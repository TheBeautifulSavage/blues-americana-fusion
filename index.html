Welcome to Dark Country Boy - Blues Americana Fusion

Dark Country Boy fuses blues and Americana traditions into something entirely new and powerfully authentic.

Dark Country Boy is an independent recording artist whose music defies easy categorization. Drawing from the deep wells of American blues, outlaw country, Americana, and folk tradition, Dark Country Boy creates music that is raw, honest, and emotionally powerful.

Fire in the Blood - Album

The debut album "Fire in the Blood" is a landmark collection of blues americana fusion music. Every track tells a story — sometimes painful, sometimes triumphant, always real.
